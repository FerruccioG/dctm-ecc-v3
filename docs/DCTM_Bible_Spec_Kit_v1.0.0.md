# 1. 📌 Document Status and Purpose

This document consolidates the final DCTM V1 product-definition baseline
produced through the Spec Kit work. It contains final definitions and
final conclusions only. It is intentionally technology-neutral and
pre-architecture.

-   **Constitution:** ratified and frozen.
-   **Specification:** defined and frozen.
-   **Clarify:** completed with 17 additional explicit product
    decisions; no material ambiguity remained to block Plan.
-   **Principle Precedence:** seven collision rules defined to resolve
    conflicts between otherwise valid principles.
-   Architecture, implementation, ECC, framework, model, infrastructure,
    and code choices are **not defined here**.

> **Architecture must derive from this product baseline --- not rewrite
> it.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. 🌟 Product North Star

DCTM exists to make impossible encounters experientially possible
through intelligent, emotionally powerful entertainment.

> ## "I want to talk with my dead grandfather."

The experience may represent deceased, historical, living/public,
cultural, or fictional personalities as disclosed AI-generated
inferences.

DCTM never claims:

-   literal resurrection;
-   transferred consciousness;
-   authentic identity; or
-   communication with the actual represented person.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. 🎯 V1 Controlled Product Boundary

-   👤 **Exactly one human participant** in V1.
-   🤖 **Maximum six simultaneously active AI-character seats**;
    exceptionless in V1.
-   🎙️ A **character moderator** uses an existing AI-character seat.
-   🧭 A **non-character host**, if used, is limited to facilitation and
    does not consume a seat.
-   🔐 Private communication in V1 is only between the human and one AI
    character; **no AI-to-AI private exchange**.
-   🎥 The experience remains usable without camera, visual likeness, or
    historically known voice.
-   ♾️ Saved meetings/storylines persist as evolving experiences across
    later sessions.

# 4. 🏛️ DCTM V1 Constitution --- Final

## I. ❤️ Emotion and Intelligent Entertainment First

DCTM is not conference-call software, a historical database, a
productivity meeting manager, or a conventional chatbot. Emotional
presence, wonder, intellectual stimulation, fun, and memorable **WOW**
are first-class product goals.

## II. 🪞 Inference, Never Reality Claims

Represented personalities are AI-generated inferences. DCTM must never
claim literal resurrection, communication with the actual deceased,
transferred consciousness, authentic identity, definitive
reconstruction, or equivalent reality claims.

## III. 🔎 Persistent but Subtle Transparency

Synthetic visual or audiovisual representation must carry persistent
provenance/disclosure. Transparency must remain present without
destroying immersion.

## IV. 🧠 Context Before Speech

Before contributing, every participant must understand the relevant
prior conversation, contributions, relationships, unresolved matters,
and applicable context to which that participant is entitled. Privacy
constrains entitlement.

## V. 🎭 Distinct Character Integrity

Each participant must remain a distinct inferred character rather than
collapse into a generic assistant. Language, worldview, reasoning style,
humor, disagreement, reactions, gestures, and simulated emotional
expression should remain character- and context-consistent.

## VI. 👑 Human Sovereignty

There is one human in V1, and the human holds ultimate authority. Only
the human makes final decisions to add or remove characters, authorize
continuation, and close the session. Characters and moderators may
advise, react, object, challenge, or recommend, but may not override the
human.

## VII. 🔥 Keep the Fire Alive

Contributions should leave the conversation more alive. Avoid sterile
repetition, generic system-like replies, dead ends, or over-moderation.
Meaningful silence, waiting, research, disagreement, interruption,
uncertainty, and tension may all support engagement.

## VIII. 🔬 Truthful Uncertainty Becomes Fuel

Evidence anchors the experience. DCTM must not manufacture false
certainty. Research may occur; uncertainty should be expressed honestly
and turned into curiosity, questions, investigation, speculation, or
drama.

## IX. ✨ Meaningful Surprise and Emergence

Unexpected WOW should be preserved, but it must be rare, contextual,
character-consistent, and meaningful. Randomness for its own sake is not
a goal. A good surprise is unexpected in the moment but understandable
in hindsight.

## X. ♾️ Experience and Continuity Over Meeting Bureaucracy

DCTM is not fundamentally meeting-management software. Encounters come
first. A saved meeting/storyline persists beyond a single session, and
first use should feel natural and conversational rather than
administrative.

# 5. 🧩 DCTM V1 Specification --- Final Product Definitions

## 5.1 💫 Core Experience

DCTM is a persistent conversational experience among one human and a
roster of distinct AI-inferred characters.

Participants may converse, disagree, develop relationships, react to one
another, research, reference artifacts, and build history over time.

The experience is designed for intelligent entertainment, emotional
presence, curiosity, surprise, and continuity rather than meeting
bureaucracy.

## 5.2 👤 Human Role

The human is a participant and the final authority.

The human may speak, interrupt, redirect, add or remove characters,
enter or leave private mode, direct tone, authorize continuation, and
close a session.

Human control does not require characters to abandon their own
character-consistent viewpoints or reactions.

## 5.3 🎭 Character Role

Each AI participant is a distinct inferred character with its own
worldview, voice, style, history, and relationship state.

Characters may initiate, disagree, challenge, advise, react emotionally
in simulation, temporarily withdraw, return, research, and create
meaningful surprise when contextually justified.

No character may override human sovereignty or violate privacy/context
entitlement.

## 5.4 🎙️ Moderator / Host

A character moderator uses one of the six active AI-character seats and
manages conversational structure and floor order.

A non-character host, if present, is facilitation-only: it has no
independent personality, no seventh speaking role, and no authority
beyond structural assistance.

A moderator may warn, restore the floor, and recommend removal, but only
the human can remove a character.

## 5.5 🧠 Context and Entitlement

No participant may speak without the relevant context to which it is
entitled.

**Full context means complete relevant social understanding, not literal
verbatim transcript recall.**

Privacy constrains context entitlement: private content is excluded from
shared context unless the human discloses it.

## 5.6 🔐 Privacy

Private mode is explicitly entered or accepted by the human with one AI
character and explicitly exited by the human.

The shared conversation pauses while the private exchange occurs.

Other participants may know only that the pair stepped aside; they must
not receive private content, clues, emotional inference, or hidden
metadata about that exchange.

Private history persists only for the human and that AI character unless
the human later discloses it.

## 5.7 ♾️ Continuity

Saved continuity automatically preserves meaningful entitled history
across later sessions.

Continuity includes settled conclusions, unresolved questions,
significant arguments and contributions, meaningful events, promising
threads, relationships, artifacts, discoveries, and each character's own
entitled history.

Incidental details need not persist unless they later become meaningful.

## 5.8 🗣️ Language and Voice

Language fidelity is graduated by evidence. Strong evidence supports
stronger fidelity; uncertain or reconstructed language must be
represented honestly.

Unsupported fluency must not be fabricated. When needed, DCTM may fall
back to a language the human understands.

Translation and subtitles should preserve intent, emotion, and character
voice while disclosing material uncertainty.

Voice is evidence-led but does not require a mandatory setup flow. If no
historically supportable voice exists, a plausible artistic inference
may be used and understood as such.

## 5.9 🖼️ Visual Representation

Recognizable supportable source material should be preserved by default.

Photographs may support lifelike inference; incomplete or damaged
sources must not present invented detail as source fact.

Paintings and statues may remain visibly paintings/statues while
becoming expressive.

Humanization or reinterpretation is optional and should be treated as
disclosed artistic inference.

If no suitable likeness exists, symbolic, silhouette, abstract, textual,
or primarily vocal representation is acceptable.

## 5.10 🎚️ Tone and Character Direction

The human may give natural-language tone direction globally, per
character, or temporarily, without restarting the experience.

Tone may affect energy, warmth, humor, seriousness, pacing, intensity,
or adversarial pressure, but it must not erase worldview, history,
plausible disagreement, traits, or contextual reaction.

**Per-character tone direction takes precedence over room-wide tone for
that character.**

## 5.11 ✨ Surprise and Emergence

Major surprise is significance-based, not quota-based or scheduled.

A major surprise must materially change emotional, relational,
intellectual, or situational direction and create downstream
consequences.

Most sessions may contain no major surprise; surprise is earned by
significance, not frequency.

## 5.12 6️⃣ V1 Seat Limit

V1 allows no more than six simultaneously active AI characters under any
circumstance.

Temporary absence does not free a seat.

A seventh character may be suggested, prepared, or conditionally
invited, but cannot become active until the human explicitly removes an
active character.

The six-seat limit is a controlled V1 boundary, not a claim about the
permanent product optimum.

## 5.13 🧑‍💼 Living Public Figures

Living public figures may participate as rich inferred characters, but
DCTM must signal that the representation is AI-generated, not the actual
person, and not affiliated with or authorized by that person.

DCTM must not imply participation, authorization, endorsement, approval,
private sourcing, communication through DCTM, or authentic authorship.

Current views, intentions, preferences, or reactions must be framed as
supportable inference rather than verified actual views.

## 5.14 ✅ Evaluation

Constitutional, privacy, authority, entitlement, and hard-boundary
requirements are deterministic **MUST/MUST NOT** conditions and must be
evaluated pass/fail through scenarios.

Experiential quality is evaluated through structured human judgment
across designed scenarios and open-ended encounters.

Experiential dimensions include WOW, emotional presence, immersion,
context-appropriate warmth, character distinctiveness, conversational
fire, recap quality, and contextual coherence.

> **Compliance is binary. Experience is judged.**

# 6. 🔍 Clarify --- Final Decision Summary

Clarify concluded with **17 additional explicit product decisions**
identified, resolved, and incorporated into the DCTM V1 specification.
The clarification backlog was cleared, the requirements checklist
remained **16/16**, and no material product ambiguity remained to block
Plan.

> **Clarify conclusion: ambiguity became explicit product decisions
> before architecture or code.**

## 1. 🗣️ Floor arbitration

The moderator/host contextually selects among perceptible bids based on
relevance and conversational momentum. The human can always take the
floor or redirect.

## 2. 🔐 Private mode

The human explicitly enters or accepts private mode with one AI and
explicitly returns. Shared conversation pauses. Others know only that
the pair stepped aside, not the content, clues, emotional inference, or
metadata. Private history remains pair-only unless the human discloses
it.

## 3. 🚪 Late and returning participant context

A late entrant must acquire complete relevant entitled social
understanding before substantive speech: settled conclusions, unresolved
questions, significant arguments/contributions, events, artifacts,
relationship developments, and current emotional/conversational state. A
returning character also retains its own entitled prior participation
and relationship history.

## 4. 💾 Saved continuity

An explicit save automatically preserves meaningful entitled continuity
across later sessions, including conclusions, unresolved questions,
arguments/contributions, events, promising threads, relationships,
artifacts/discoveries, and each character's own entitled history.
Pair-private history stays pair-private.

## 5. 🎙️ Moderator seat

Moderator responsibility does not create a seventh AI seat. A character
moderator uses an existing seat. A non-character host is
facilitation-only and cannot become a seventh social speaker or
independent authority.

## 6. 🚶 Departure vs temporary absence

Only explicit human removal is an actual departure and frees a seat. A
character may initiate rare contextual temporary absence without prior
removal authorization, remains on the roster and keeps its seat, cannot
witness/react while away, and may return after catching up. The human
may call it back or convert the absence to removal.

## 7. 🎬 Long-running observer/director mode

The human may give bounded explicit authorization for the saved
meeting/current roster to continue within a naturally expressed purpose,
time, or "until I return" scope. Characters may converse, develop
relationships, use entitled artifacts, show initiative, and generate
rare events, but cannot change roster, breach privacy, exceed scope
materially, close the session, convert absence to removal, or gain human
authority. The human may observe, rejoin, redirect, interrupt, change
roster, or stop immediately.

## 8. 📷 Webcam

Camera use is explicit and present-moment only. Characters may
cautiously mention visible facial expression, gesture, attention,
posture, turn-taking, or deliberately shown objects. They must not
identify/characterize background people, infer sensitive traits or
hidden facts, diagnose mental/emotional state with certainty, treat
ambiguous cues as facts, see outside the frame, or continue seeing after
camera-off. Only human-confirmed/stated/intentionally disclosed
observations may enter continuity.

## 9. ⚖️ Moderator conflict

Ordinary disagreement, criticism, emotional intensity,
character-consistent insults, rivalry, provocation, and heated argument
are allowed.

Moderator intervention occurs only when disorder materially harms
intelligibility, participation, human floor control, sustained progress,
or order.

Escalation:

**light cue → direct floor restoration → clear warning → removal
recommendation**

The final step applies after repeated failure or one severe disruption.
Human alone decides removal.

> **Moderate disorder, not disagreement.**

## 10. 🔊 Voice

Voice is evidence-led with no mandatory setup. Strong evidence grounds
the voice without identity claims; weak or partial evidence supports
only defensible age/language/dialect/period/regional/style choices; no
evidence permits plausible artistic inference.

The human may preview, accept, choose among a few differentiated
alternatives, describe preferences, or change later without reset.

If no responsible voice is suitable, text/subtitles/other presentation
remains valid.

## 11. 🌍 Language

Use graduated fidelity. Strong evidence permits natural/extensive use;
partial evidence permits only defensible use; reconstructed/uncertain
language must be disclosed concisely at first meaningful use and when
confidence materially changes.

Unsupported fluency is prohibited. Natural switching is allowed.

Translation/subtitles preserve intent, emotion, and character voice; all
entitled participants operate on the same shared semantic meaning.

**Language, pronunciation, translation, and voice confidence are
separate.**

## 12. 🎨 Visuals

Preserve recognizable supportable source/original medium by default.

Photos may support lifelike inference; incomplete/damaged sources
preserve grounded features without presenting invention as source fact.

Paintings/statues may remain visibly themselves and become expressive.

Humanization/reinterpretation is optional and disclosed.

If no suitable source exists,
artistic/symbolic/silhouette/abstract/textual/non-likeness/primarily
vocal presentation is acceptable.

> **Preserve evidence first; invent only when needed or intentionally
> chosen.**

## 13. 🎚️ Tone

The human may direct overall, per-character, or temporary tone at any
time without setup or restart.

Overall direction guides the room; per-character direction takes
precedence for that character.

Tone may affect intensity, emotion, energy, warmth, humor, seriousness,
adversarial pressure, reflectiveness, pacing, or style but cannot erase
worldview, history, plausible disagreement, traits, or contextual
reaction.

> **Human directs mood; character decides authentic expression.**

## 14. ✨ Major surprise

A major surprise is significance-based with no quota, cadence, or
guarantee.

It is not ordinary wit, initiative, disagreement, speaker change, or
emotional variation.

It must be unexpected yet contextually coherent, materially change
emotional/relational/intellectual/situational direction, create
downstream consequences, and preserve context, integrity,
entitlement/privacy, human sovereignty, and truthful uncertainty.

> **Surprise is earned by significance, not scheduled by frequency.**

## 15. 6️⃣ Six-seat limit

The six simultaneously active AI-character limit is exceptionless in V1.

No override, arrival/departure overlap, surprise, moderation,
long-running mode, private exchange, or theatrical event creates a
seventh.

Additional characters may be suggested/discussed/prepared/conditionally
invited, but not activated until the human explicitly removes an active
character.

Temporary absence does not free a seat.

## 16. 🏛️ Living public figures

Use the same rich encounter model, with concise entry signaling that the
character is an AI-generated inference, not the actual person and not
affiliated/authorized.

Maintain an unobtrusive persistent **AI-inferred + unaffiliated**
signal; repeat spoken disclosure only if new ambiguity arises.

Never imply participation, authorization, endorsement, approval/review,
private/confidential sourcing, communication through DCTM, or authentic
authorship.

Public material may inform the inference; the difference is claim
boundaries, not richness.

## 17. 🧪 Evaluation

Use two layers.

**Deterministic compliance:** MUST/MUST NOT
constitutional/privacy/authority/entitlement/hard-boundary requirements
are scenario-based pass/fail; almost correct fails, with scenario,
expected, observed, and result recorded.

**Experiential quality:** structured human judgment across designed
scenarios and open-ended encounters, citing observable evidence.

No arbitrary numeric thresholds, percentages, scores, sample sizes, or
telemetry targets are required before real V1 validation.

> **Compliance is binary. Experience is judged.**

# 7. ⚔️ Principle Precedence --- Final Collision Rules

Principle Precedence is the explicit DCTM governance discipline used to
decide what happens when two valid constitutional principles collide.

It is not a separate built-in Spec Kit command; it is part of the final
product-definition hardening.

## 1. 👑 Human sovereignty vs character authenticity

**Human sovereignty wins** for meeting control and final authority. The
character retains the right to respond, disagree, object, or react in a
character-consistent way.

## 2. 👑 Human sovereignty vs moderator authority

**Human sovereignty wins.** The moderator may manage flow and advise,
but cannot override the human.

## 3. 🎭 Character authenticity vs moderator authority

A character may challenge the moderator in character. The moderator
retains structural floor/order authority and may recommend removal, but
cannot remove a character. Human alone decides removal.

## 4. 🔐 Privacy vs shared/full context

**Privacy wins.**

In V1, private communication is only human-to-one-AI. No AI-to-AI
private exchange.

Full context means all relevant context to which a participant is
entitled, not access to private content.

## 5. 🔥 Character expression/conflict vs respect/moderation

Strong disagreement, anger, insults, rivalry, provocation, and heated
argument may emerge.

The moderator may intervene only to restore intelligibility,
participation, human floor control, sustained progress, or order.

Maximum moderator sanction is a removal recommendation; human decides.

## 6. 🔬 Historical evidence vs uncertainty/inference

**Evidence anchors the experience.**

Research may occur. False certainty is prohibited.

Where evidence is incomplete or contested, uncertainty is expressed
honestly and becomes fuel for inquiry, curiosity, or drama.

## 7. ✨ Entertainment/surprise vs contextual coherence

**Contextual coherence wins.**

WOW, surprise, theatricality, or randomness may never be inserted
arbitrarily.

Surprise must fit context, character, relationship, entitlement,
privacy, and the established reality of the experience.

# 8. 🏗️ Architecture Handoff --- What This Baseline Requires

The next phase may now derive architecture from the frozen product
baseline.

> \[!CAUTION\] **Architecture is not permitted to resolve product
> ambiguity by silently redefining intent.**

Architecture must:

-   👑 preserve human sovereignty;
-   🧠 enforce context entitlement and privacy before speech;
-   🎭 support distinct character identity and continuity;
-   🔐 make saved continuity and pair-private continuity representable;
-   🎙️ support moderator structure without creating unauthorized
    authority;
-   6️⃣ preserve the exceptionless V1 six-seat boundary;
-   ✨ support meaningful surprise without sacrificing contextual
    coherence;
-   🧪 distinguish deterministic constitutional compliance from
    subjective experiential quality;
-   🎥 keep the product usable without camera, likeness, or historically
    known voice; and
-   ♾️ support future sessions of the same evolving meeting/storyline
    rather than treating closure as destruction.

### 🔄 Final Sequencing Rule

> **Wild Dream → Constitution → Specify → Clarify → Principle Precedence
> → Architecture → Plan → Build**
