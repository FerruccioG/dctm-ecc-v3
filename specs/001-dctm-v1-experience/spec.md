# Feature Specification: DCTM V1 Experience

**Feature Branch**: `main` (no branch-creation hook configured)

**Created**: 2026-09-03

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Create the baseline product specification for DCTM V1 Experience: an emotionally powerful, disclosed AI-inference entertainment experience that begins with one human and one character and can evolve into an intimate multi-character encounter."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Begin an Impossible Encounter (Priority: P1)

A first-time human says whom they want to encounter, such as a deceased grandfather, and enters a one-to-one experience without configuring a formal meeting. The inferred character appears with expressive visual presence, animation, voice, appropriate language or language flavor, and a contextual, relationship-aware salutation. A persistent but unobtrusive signal identifies the character as an AI-generated inference for entertainment.

**Why this priority**: This is the north-star experience and establishes whether DCTM feels like an emotionally meaningful encounter rather than a conventional application or chatbot.

**Independent Test**: A participant can request an encounter with a grandfather using suitable source material and experience the opening through the first conversational exchange; observers can verify the disclosure, salutation qualities, character distinctiveness, and absence of forced meeting setup.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a first-time human requests to talk with their grandfather, **When** the inferred character becomes ready, **Then** the encounter opens one-to-one with an expressive visual, animated, vocal, linguistic, and emotionally contextual salutation rather than an application startup or meeting-configuration sequence.
2. **Given** relationship, language, dialect, and relevant time-of-day context are available, **When** the character greets the human, **Then** the salutation reflects that context without using a mechanically fixed phrase.
3. **Given** some salutation context is unavailable or uncertain, **When** the encounter opens, **Then** the character uses only supportable context, avoids fabricated certainty, and still provides a warm, character-consistent greeting.
4. **Given** any synthetic visual or audiovisual representation is visible, **When** the human views the encounter, **Then** a persistent, subtle disclosure communicates that it is an AI-generated inference for entertainment without repeatedly interrupting the encounter.
5. **Given** limited source imagery such as a portrait, painting, or statue, **When** the character appears, **Then** that source can remain the recognizable visual identity and its limitations do not automatically block the encounter.

### User Story 2 - Sustain a Living One-to-One Conversation (Priority: P1)

The human converses with a character who understands the living context, remains recognizably distinct, responds to interruption and provocation in character, allows thinking time, and turns uncertainty or a need for investigation into an engaging continuation rather than a dead end.

**Why this priority**: The opening WOW has value only if the character can sustain emotional and intellectual presence afterward.

**Independent Test**: Conduct a one-to-one conversation containing a long human pause, interruption, provocation, repeated topic, period-inappropriate question, and unresolved factual question; verify that every response remains contextual, truthful, differentiated, and forward-moving.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an established emotional tone, **When** conversation continues, **Then** the character builds on that tone through context-consistent warmth, contemplation, tension, humor, seriousness, or intellectual intensity rather than reverting to generic assistance.
2. **Given** the human pauses to think, **When** the pause becomes noticeable, **Then** no hard conversational timeout punishes the human and any acknowledgement of the pause is warm and character-consistent.
3. **Given** the human interrupts a speaking character, **When** the interruption occurs, **Then** the character reacts according to its personality and relationship with the human, while yielding the floor if the human insists.
4. **Given** the human challenges, teases, insults, or provokes a character, **When** the character responds, **Then** its response reflects personality, context, relationship state, and provocation intensity rather than a universal canned response.
5. **Given** a question exceeds the character's available grounding, **When** further investigation or deliberation could help, **Then** the character first acknowledges what it can support, naturally requests time, remains experientially present during the pause, and returns with a character-consistent synthesis.
6. **Given** investigation does not resolve the question, **When** the character returns, **Then** it communicates uncertainty truthfully and uses it to create a question, qualified interpretation, shared investigation, disagreement, or another meaningful path forward.
7. **Given** a historical character encounters modern information outside its lived period, **When** it responds, **Then** it recognizes the temporal boundary and interprets the information through its own supportable worldview rather than claiming it as original lived knowledge.

### User Story 3 - Expand the Encounter Naturally (Priority: P1)

After the one-to-one encounter has emotional footing, either the character or the human may conversationally propose inviting another historical, deceased, distant or living public, cultural, or imagined character. Only the human authorizes the invitation. The invited character arrives as a social event, absorbs entitled context, and adds to the existing conversation.

**Why this priority**: Natural expansion from intimacy to an impossible ensemble is the defining V1 arc.

**Independent Test**: Begin with one character, invite a second after substantive conversation, and verify authorization, arrival, acknowledgement, contextual admission, and additive first contribution without reset or administrative participant setup.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an established one-to-one encounter, **When** expansion becomes contextually appropriate, **Then** the character may invite the human to imagine whom to add in conversational language that communicates the broad character possibilities without presenting a participant form.
2. **Given** the human or a character suggests a participant, **When** no human authorization has been given, **Then** no participant is added.
3. **Given** the human authorizes an invitation and grounding is not ready, **When** the invitation is handled, **Then** the experience may offer to prepare the inferred character without requiring administrative setup from the human.
4. **Given** an invited character is ready, **When** it enters, **Then** it appears, gives an in-character salutation, and is naturally welcomed or acknowledged by existing participants.
5. **Given** conversation predates the arrival, **When** the new character first contributes, **Then** it has absorbed all relevant non-private context to which it is entitled, naturally demonstrates understanding of where the discussion has reached, and adds new value without a fixed acknowledgement phrase.
6. **Given** an active conversation, **When** another character arrives, **Then** the conversation and relationship history continue without resetting.

### User Story 4 - Experience an Intimate Multi-Character Room (Priority: P1)

The human engages with as many as six simultaneously active AI characters who converse with the human and one another, remain socially present when silent, request and yield the floor intelligibly, disagree or form affinities, and sometimes initiate relevant moves. A moderator or equivalent host may protect conversational flow but never overrides the human.

**Why this priority**: The ensemble must feel like a socially alive impossible encounter, not a grid of interchangeable prompt-response endpoints.

**Independent Test**: Run a six-character encounter containing simultaneous interest in speaking, interruption, disagreement with the moderator, non-verbal reactions, character initiative, and a human control decision; verify coherence, distinctness, moderation limits, and human sovereignty.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** multiple active characters, **When** any character contributes, **Then** it understands its entitled living context, recognizes relevant prior statements, avoids unnecessary repetition, and adds a new angle, meaning, challenge, question, interpretation, or emotional development whenever possible.
2. **Given** a character returns to a prior point, **When** it speaks again, **Then** the point has meaningful evolution rather than duplicating its earlier response.
3. **Given** a character is silent, **When** context calls for a reaction, **Then** it may show character-consistent attention, expression, posture, gesture, disagreement, curiosity, or a request for the floor instead of disconnected decorative looping.
4. **Given** another participant notices a meaningful non-verbal reaction, **When** it is socially appropriate, **Then** that participant may acknowledge the reaction or invite the reacting character to speak.
5. **Given** a character has an unprompted but relevant conversational move, **When** it takes initiative, **Then** it may ask, challenge, request permission, propose a thread, or suggest a participant without taking control from the human.
6. **Given** conflict escalates or turn-taking becomes disorderly, **When** the moderator intervenes, **Then** the intervention is expressive and understandable and may request order, manage the floor, or recommend removal, but cannot remove a participant or close the session.
7. **Given** a character plausibly challenges or disrespects the moderator, **When** conflict occurs, **Then** character expression remains possible and the moderator retains only its allowed flow-management authority.
8. **Given** the room already has six active AI characters, **When** another invitation is proposed, **Then** the experience explains conversationally that an active seat must first be freed and does not add a seventh active AI character.

### User Story 5 - Shape Tone Without Owning Character Reactions (Priority: P2)

The human can influence an encounter toward a mode such as warm, humorous, reflective, adversarial, technical, philosophical, or serious without completing tedious setup. Characters preserve their distinct personalities within that direction and may receive contrasting directions where appropriate.

**Why this priority**: Human direction broadens replay value while character integrity preserves the encounter's credibility and surprise.

**Independent Test**: Apply an overall emotional direction and contrasting per-character directions during an encounter; verify recognizable influence on tone without identical behavior or forced agreement.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an encounter is beginning or underway, **When** the human expresses a desired emotional mode conversationally, **Then** subsequent behavior visibly reflects that mode without requiring a lengthy configuration flow.
2. **Given** a requested mode, **When** different characters respond within it, **Then** each retains its vocabulary, worldview, humor, reasoning style, and relationship-dependent reactions.
3. **Given** the human requests contrasting tonal directions for different characters, **When** the directions are compatible with product boundaries, **Then** the contrast becomes observable without making either character generic or mechanically compliant.

### User Story 6 - Collaborate Through Artifacts and Language (Priority: P2)

The human can introduce relevant photographs, letters, diaries, documents, recordings, or historical records. Characters receive them as meaningful events, react in context, and incorporate supportable discoveries. Characters may use appropriate languages or dialects, with translation support where needed, while the shared conversation stays coherent.

**Why this priority**: Artifacts and language turn the human into a collaborator in discovery and deepen character presence beyond question answering.

**Independent Test**: Introduce a relevant family letter and an artifact whose meaning is uncertain during a multilingual encounter; verify contextual reactions, honest evidence treatment, translation availability, and coherent cross-language references.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an active encounter, **When** the human contributes a relevant artifact, **Then** its introduction is acknowledged as a meaningful conversational event and entitled characters react to it in the current context.
2. **Given** an artifact provides new evidence, **When** characters discuss it, **Then** they distinguish supported observations from inference and may revise earlier interpretations or relationship dynamics.
3. **Given** an artifact remains ambiguous, **When** characters respond, **Then** they preserve uncertainty and use it for curiosity or investigation rather than inventing a definitive account.
4. **Given** adequately supported language evidence, **When** a character speaks, **Then** it may use an associated language, dialect, or reconstructed historical language while avoiding certainty claims beyond the evidence.
5. **Given** the human does not understand a character's visible language, **When** translation is needed, **Then** subtitles or an equivalent translation can make the contribution understandable without breaking conversational continuity.
6. **Given** characters use different visible languages, **When** they interact, **Then** each still responds coherently to the shared entitled meaning of the conversation.

### User Story 7 - Communicate Privately (Priority: P2)

The human can communicate privately with one AI character. Other AI characters do not receive that content solely because they otherwise receive shared context. The human may later disclose it.

**Why this priority**: Intimacy and privacy are necessary for sensitive encounters and constitutionally override indiscriminate context sharing.

**Independent Test**: Exchange information privately with one character, return to the shared room, and verify that other characters neither reference nor act on it until the human explicitly discloses it.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** multiple AI characters are active, **When** the human enters private communication with one character, **Then** only that human-character pair participates in and receives the private content.
2. **Given** private content exists, **When** shared conversation resumes, **Then** other AI characters do not reference or behave as though they know that content.
3. **Given** the human later chooses to disclose private information, **When** it enters shared context, **Then** entitled characters may incorporate it from that point onward.
4. **Given** one AI character seeks private communication with another AI character, **When** the request would create an AI-to-AI private exchange, **Then** the experience does not permit it in V1.

---

### User Story 8 - Direct Arrivals, Departures, and Closure (Priority: P2)

The human alone controls whether AI characters join or leave and when a session closes. Departures and session goodbyes become emotionally meaningful moments rather than administrative deletion or abrupt termination.

**Why this priority**: Human sovereignty must remain visible while control events support rather than diminish the entertainment.

**Independent Test**: Have a character request another's removal, decline it, later remove a character, replace the freed seat, and close the session; verify that only human decisions have control effects and every transition preserves continuity.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a character or moderator recommends removal, **When** the human does not authorize it, **Then** the targeted character remains active.
2. **Given** an AI character wishes to leave independently, **When** the human has not directed departure, **Then** the character does not remove itself from the active experience.
3. **Given** the human directs a character to leave, **When** departure occurs, **Then** the character may offer a character-consistent thanks, final thought, challenge, reminder, or relational acknowledgement before its seat becomes available.
4. **Given** a departure frees a seat, **When** the human authorizes a replacement, **Then** the incoming character follows normal arrival and contextual-admission behavior and the conversation does not reset.
5. **Given** a character or moderator identifies a natural stopping point, **When** the human chooses to continue, **Then** the session remains open.
6. **Given** the human closes a session, **When** participants say goodbye, **Then** closure conveys reflection or anticipation of continuation without implying that a saved meeting or relationship has been destroyed.

### User Story 9 - Resume an Evolving Saved Meeting (Priority: P2)

The human explicitly saves a meeting and later begins another session in the same evolving storyline. A concise, conversational recap identifies conclusions, unresolved questions, discoveries, relationship developments, and promising threads, then invites continuation, new topics, or participant suggestions.

**Why this priority**: Cross-session accumulation turns encounters into evolving relationships and ensures the room remembers without becoming bureaucratic.

**Independent Test**: Save and close a meeting after conclusions, conflict, a private exchange, and open questions; resume it and verify accurate orientation, privacy preservation, relational continuity, and freedom to continue or redirect.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a session contains meaningful developments and the human explicitly saves the meeting, **When** the session closes, **Then** conclusions, unresolved questions, discoveries, relationship developments, and promising future threads are available to support later continuity.
2. **Given** a saved meeting is resumed, **When** the new session opens, **Then** a moderator or equivalent host gives a short conversational recap of what was settled, what remains open, and meaningful developments without presenting a corporate agenda.
3. **Given** a resumed session opens, **When** the recap concludes, **Then** the human may continue a prior thread, add a new topic, or consider a suggested participant.
4. **Given** relationships changed through verbal or meaningful non-verbal interaction in prior saved sessions, **When** characters meet again, **Then** later responses reflect the applicable trust, affection, respect, curiosity, rivalry, suspicion, resentment, hostility, reconciliation, grudging respect, or combinations thereof.
5. **Given** prior relationships were friendly or hostile, **When** new events occur, **Then** evolution may move in either direction and is not automatically pushed toward friendship.
6. **Given** a prior private human-character exchange was not disclosed, **When** the meeting resumes, **Then** unauthorized characters remain unaware of it.

---

### User Story 10 - Choose Persistence and Recording Separately (Priority: P2)

The human decides whether an encounter becomes a continuing saved meeting and, where recording is supported, decides separately whether to retain an audiovisual replay. An unsaved encounter may begin again from a clean slate.

**Why this priority**: Explicit choice protects human control and distinguishes experiential continuity from replay media.

**Independent Test**: Complete one unsaved encounter and one saved-but-unrecorded encounter; verify clean-slate behavior for the former and cross-session continuity without required audiovisual replay for the latter.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an encounter is not explicitly saved, **When** the human later recreates it, **Then** it may start from a clean slate without automatically inheriting the prior encounter's meeting or relationship history.
2. **Given** the human explicitly saves a meeting but does not choose recording, **When** it resumes, **Then** meaningful continuity is available without requiring a full audiovisual replay.
3. **Given** recording is supported, **When** the human chooses whether to record, **Then** that choice is separate from whether the meeting preserves continuity.
4. **Given** a saved meeting has completed several sessions, **When** another session closes, **Then** the meeting remains eligible for later continuation without an arbitrary V1 lifetime session count.

---

### User Story 11 - Observe a Long-Running Encounter (Priority: P3)

The human may explicitly authorize AI characters to continue interacting for an extended period without constant human participation, observe or direct the encounter, leave, and later return to meaningful preserved evolution.

**Why this priority**: Observer/director mode extends DCTM from conversation into evolving entertainment while retaining human authorization and control.

**Independent Test**: Authorize an active saved ensemble to continue while the human is absent, return after multiple developments, and verify reviewable continuity, preserved meaningful state, and the human's ability to intervene or close.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a saved encounter is active, **When** the human explicitly authorizes extended AI interaction, **Then** characters may continue contextually without requiring constant human prompts.
2. **Given** the human has not authorized extended continuation, **When** the human stops participating, **Then** the experience does not silently enter long-running autonomous continuation.
3. **Given** an authorized long-running encounter evolves, **When** the human later returns, **Then** the human can understand or review the meaningful developments and resume observer or director participation.
4. **Given** interruption occurs during a long-running saved encounter, **When** the human returns, **Then** already preserved meaningful evolution is not entirely lost.
5. **Given** long-running interaction is active, **When** the human directs a participant change or closes the session, **Then** human authority takes effect.

### Edge Cases

- The requested character has sparse, contradictory, or no reliable personal source material; the experience must identify the limits of the inference, avoid authenticity claims, and offer an honest, creatively bounded encounter rather than inventing certainty.
- The only visual source is a face, bust, statue, painting, or damaged image; this must not automatically block participation, and the representation must not claim detail unsupported by the source.
- No authentic voice evidence exists; the experience may provide a plausible artistic voice but must not label it historically authentic.
- A requested dialect, historical language, or translation is not adequately supported; the experience must disclose the limitation, use a clear fallback when available, and preserve coherence without overstating accuracy.
- A seventh active AI character is requested; no seventh seat becomes active until the human frees one of the six seats.
- Several AI characters simultaneously want the floor; the experience must keep the contenders intelligible, avoid uncontrolled overlap, and preserve the human's ability to take the floor, while the exact resolution convention remains for Clarify.
- A character repeatedly restates a position; later contributions must evolve its purpose, evidence, perspective, or emotional meaning, or the floor should move elsewhere.
- A meaningful silence lasts an unusually long time; characters may remain present or acknowledge it warmly but must not convert the pause into a punitive timeout.
- Investigation takes time or fails; the pause remains non-disruptive, the eventual response separates known information from inference, and unresolved uncertainty produces a viable continuation.
- The human introduces an artifact that contradicts prior grounding; characters must acknowledge the conflict, revise supportable interpretations, and avoid retroactive false certainty.
- The human privately reveals information relevant to the shared topic; other characters remain uninformed unless the human discloses it, even if omission limits their contributions.
- A character insults the moderator or another participant; the moderator may intervene or recommend removal, but only the human's decision changes active participation.
- The human interrupts a character that resists yielding; the reaction may remain character-consistent, but a repeated human claim to the floor prevails.
- The human asks a character to depart during conflict; the departure may be emotionally charged but must occur and must not erase prior shared context.
- A saved meeting is resumed with a different active roster; returning and new participants receive only entitled context, while the recap remains understandable to the human.
- An unsaved encounter is recreated with the same characters; it may begin from a clean slate and must not imply remembered relationships that depended on the discarded encounter.
- A surprise event would be dramatic but lacks contextual justification; it must not occur merely to manufacture entertainment.
- Optional camera input is unavailable, denied, or disabled; the complete encounter remains usable and characters do not claim to observe the human visually.
- Visible human reactions are ambiguous; characters may respond tentatively when observation is authorized but must not state uncertain interpretation as fact.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

#### Experience Foundation and Transparency

- **FR-001**: DCTM V1 MUST support exactly one human participant in an encounter and MUST NOT enable multi-human participation.
- **FR-002**: Every new first-time encounter MUST begin with exactly one active AI character and the human, without forcing a corporate agenda or formal meeting-configuration flow.
- **FR-003**: The opening MUST present the initial character through expressive visual presence, animation, voice, language, and an emotionally contextual salutation.
- **FR-004**: When supportable context is available, the salutation MUST reflect relevant time of day, known relationship, appropriate language, and appropriate dialect or language flavor; unavailable context MUST NOT be fabricated.
- **FR-005**: The opening MUST feel like the first moment of an encounter and MUST NOT foreground application startup mechanics.
- **FR-006**: Every represented personality MUST be identified as an AI-generated inference for entertainment and MUST NOT be presented as literal resurrection, transferred consciousness, authentic identity, communication with the actual person, or definitive historical reconstruction.
- **FR-007**: A persistent, subtle inference/synthetic disclosure MUST remain visible throughout synthetic visual and audiovisual representation without repeatedly interrupting the experience.
- **FR-008**: DCTM MUST prioritize emotional presence, fascination, wonder, intellectual stimulation, fun, and memorable moments over administrative meeting mechanics.
- **FR-009**: After the opening, behavior MUST maintain or meaningfully evolve the established emotional tone rather than revert to generic assistant behavior.

#### Character Integrity, Context, and Conversation

- **FR-010**: Each AI participant MUST remain a distinct inferred character whose language, vocabulary, worldview, reasoning style, humor, disagreement, reactions, interruption behavior, gestures, uncertainty, and social behavior are consistent with both character and context.
- **FR-011**: Before every contribution, each AI participant MUST understand all relevant current and prior conversational, deliberative, relational, and unresolved context to which that participant is entitled.
- **FR-012**: Every AI contribution SHOULD recognize what has already been said, avoid unnecessary repetition, and add a new angle, meaning, challenge, question, interpretation, or emotional development whenever possible.
- **FR-013**: A return to an earlier point MUST introduce meaningful evolution in perspective, purpose, evidence, argument, context, or emotional significance.
- **FR-014**: Contributions MUST avoid generic filler, mechanical dead ends, unnecessary system language, robotic uniformity, and abrupt momentum-killing responses.
- **FR-015**: Meaningful silence, contemplation, disagreement, reflection, tension, or uncertainty MUST be permitted as ways to sustain vitality; DCTM MUST NOT equate conversational fire with constant noise or stimulation.
- **FR-016**: AI characters MAY initiate context- and character-consistent moves, including questions, challenges, requests to pursue a point, proposed threads, or participant suggestions, without acquiring human control authority.
- **FR-017**: AI characters MUST be able to indicate a desire to contribute without immediately taking over the conversation; the exact verbal or non-verbal mechanism is deferred to Clarify.
- **FR-018**: Non-speaking characters SHOULD display socially relevant non-verbal presence, and those signals MAY influence the conversation when noticed by others.
- **FR-019**: Non-verbal behavior MUST remain context-consistent and MUST NOT degrade into repetitive decorative loops disconnected from the living conversation.

#### Human Pace, Interruption, and Provocation

- **FR-020**: The human MUST NOT lose participation or be subjected to a hard conversational timeout solely for thinking slowly.
- **FR-021**: A character MAY acknowledge a noticeable human pause, but the acknowledgement MUST be warm, non-mechanical, and character-consistent.
- **FR-022**: The human MUST be able to interrupt an AI participant.
- **FR-023**: An interrupted character MUST respond in a character- and context-consistent way and MUST NOT use a universal canned interruption response.
- **FR-024**: A character MAY initially yield, ask to finish, or react negatively to interruption, but MUST yield the floor when the human insists.
- **FR-025**: Responses to human challenge, insult, teasing, provocation, or strong disagreement MUST vary according to character personality, current context, relationship state, and the nature and intensity of the provocation.
- **FR-026**: Human control over the encounter MUST NOT require a character to agree with the human or suppress its character-consistent simulated reaction.

#### Investigation, Evidence, and Temporal Boundaries

- **FR-027**: When a question exceeds currently available grounding, a character MAY identify what it can support and naturally request time to investigate or deliberate rather than lead with a cold conversational dead end.
- **FR-028**: An investigation or deliberation pause SHOULD preserve entertainment through character-consistent contemplation, ambience, or another non-disruptive experiential behavior.
- **FR-029**: After investigation, the character MUST integrate existing grounding, newly found relevant information, and character-consistent interpretation while distinguishing supported information from inference.
- **FR-030**: If uncertainty remains, the character MUST state it truthfully and MUST NOT manufacture false certainty to sustain entertainment.
- **FR-031**: Unresolved uncertainty SHOULD create a viable continuation through curiosity, questions, investigation, explicitly qualified speculation, disagreement, shared discovery, or drama.
- **FR-032**: Characters MUST recognize meaningful temporal, experiential, and domain boundaries and MUST NOT casually present later or outside knowledge as part of their original lived knowledge.
- **FR-033**: A character facing information outside its scope MAY acknowledge the boundary, refer to or suggest a better-positioned participant, or interpret newly introduced information through its own worldview.

#### Invitations, Arrival, and Active Seats

- **FR-034**: After the initial one-to-one encounter is established, the human MUST be able to request additional AI participants conversationally.
- **FR-035**: AI characters MAY suggest or conversationally invite consideration of historical, deceased, distant or living public, cultural, or imagined characters within applicable boundaries, but suggestions MUST NOT add a participant.
- **FR-036**: Only the human MAY authorize adding an AI participant.
- **FR-037**: When grounding material is not ready, DCTM MAY offer to prepare an inferred character without requiring the human to complete administrative participant setup.
- **FR-038**: An arriving character MUST appear, provide an in-character salutation, and receive natural acknowledgement from one or more existing participants when socially appropriate.
- **FR-039**: Before its first substantive contribution, a late entrant MUST absorb all relevant meeting context to which it is entitled.
- **FR-040**: A late entrant's first contribution MUST naturally demonstrate understanding of the conversation so far and add something new; no exact acknowledgement wording may be required.
- **FR-041**: Arrival or replacement MUST NOT reset the active conversation, conclusions, open questions, or applicable relationship context.
- **FR-042**: DCTM V1 MUST allow no more than six simultaneously active AI characters; this limit applies to active seats, not necessarily to all saved or historical character profiles.
- **FR-043**: When six seats are active, an additional character MUST NOT become active until the human directs a departure that frees a seat.

#### Moderator and Multi-Character Flow

- **FR-044**: A multi-character encounter MAY assign a moderator or equivalent host responsibility to preserve intelligibility, support turn-taking, protect conversational vitality, manage escalating disorder, frame resumptions, and support contextual entrances and exits.
- **FR-045**: The moderator MUST NOT own the meeting, add or remove participants, override human control, or close a session.
- **FR-046**: Characters MAY strongly disagree with or disrespect the moderator when plausible for character, context, and relationship.
- **FR-047**: When boundaries are crossed, the moderator MAY signal the issue, request order, manage the floor, and strongly recommend removal to the human.
- **FR-048**: A moderator recommendation MUST have no automatic removal effect.
- **FR-049**: When multiple characters seek the floor, DCTM MUST keep participation intelligible, prevent uncontrolled conversational overlap, and preserve the human's right to take the floor; exact arbitration is deferred to Clarify.
- **FR-050**: First-session topic framing SHOULD be natural and dramatic rather than a corporate agenda; resumed sessions MAY use lightweight continuity framing.

#### Human-Directed Tone, Language, Visuals, and Camera

- **FR-051**: The human MUST be able to influence an encounter's emotional mode through an interaction that does not require tedious formal setup.
- **FR-052**: Supported modes MUST be capable of including humorous, reflective, adversarial, technical, philosophical, warm, and serious directions.
- **FR-053**: DCTM MAY apply different human-requested tonal directions to different characters, but every character MUST retain its distinct personality within the direction.
- **FR-054**: Character visual presence MUST be able to use the best suitable available source, including a photograph, portrait, painting, statue, or other visual representation.
- **FR-055**: A non-photographic source MAY remain the character's visual identity and be expressively animated rather than automatically transformed into a photorealistic human.
- **FR-056**: Missing body or hand imagery MUST NOT automatically prevent participation; exact visual handling is deferred to Clarify.
- **FR-057**: Each character SHOULD have an appropriate inferred voice without requiring lengthy technical configuration by the human.
- **FR-058**: When authentic voice evidence is absent, DCTM MUST identify the voice as plausible artistic inference and MUST NOT claim historical voice authenticity.
- **FR-059**: Language MUST be treated as a first-class element of character presence, including supported associated languages, dialects, and responsibly reconstructed historical languages.
- **FR-060**: Historical or reconstructed language behavior MUST NOT imply linguistic certainty beyond available support.
- **FR-061**: The human MUST be able to receive subtitles or equivalent translation when needed, while all participants continue to understand entitled shared conversational meaning across visible languages.
- **FR-062**: Human camera participation MUST remain optional, expressly human-controlled, and unnecessary for a functional encounter.
- **FR-063**: When the human voluntarily enables camera interplay, characters MAY respond naturally to visible reactions or gestures but MUST NOT treat ambiguous interpretation as certain fact.

#### Artifacts and Emergent Human Expertise

- **FR-064**: The human MUST be able to introduce relevant photographs, letters, diaries, documents, recordings, historical records, and comparable real-world artifacts during an encounter.
- **FR-065**: Introduction of a new artifact MUST receive context-appropriate acknowledgement and SHOULD feel like a meaningful event rather than administrative upload handling.
- **FR-066**: Entitled characters MUST react to contributed artifacts in the living context and MUST distinguish evidence, ambiguity, and inference.
- **FR-067**: New artifact evidence MAY change interpretations, conversation threads, and relationship state.
- **FR-068**: DCTM V1 MUST NOT require an extensive pre-encounter human expertise profile.
- **FR-069**: Characters MAY infer the human's relevant expertise from what the human naturally demonstrates and, within the applicable saved meeting context, later recognize the human as better positioned to answer a question.

#### Privacy

- **FR-070**: DCTM V1 MUST support private communication between the one human and one selected AI character.
- **FR-071**: Private human-character content MUST NOT be disclosed to other AI participants merely to satisfy shared-context requirements.
- **FR-072**: The human MUST be able to disclose previously private information later, after which it MAY become part of entitled shared context.
- **FR-073**: DCTM V1 MUST NOT support private AI-to-AI communication.
- **FR-074**: Entry to and exit from private communication MUST make the current privacy state understandable to the human; the exact interaction is deferred to Clarify.

#### Departure and Human Sovereignty

- **FR-075**: Only the human MAY make the final decision to add or remove an AI character, authorize extended continuation, or close a session.
- **FR-076**: AI characters MUST NOT independently remove themselves from the active participant roster in V1. This restriction governs actual departure or removal that frees an active seat; it does not by itself prohibit a rare, contextually coherent temporary in-world pause or brief absence that leaves the character on the active roster and does not free its seat.
- **FR-077**: Characters and moderators MAY suggest that a participant leave, but such speech MUST have no automatic control effect.
- **FR-078**: When the human directs a character to leave, the departure MUST occur and SHOULD include a socially and emotionally meaningful, character-consistent closing contribution.
- **FR-079**: A departing character MAY thank participants, summarize a final thought, offer a challenge or reminder, or acknowledge relationships and unresolved matters.
- **FR-080**: After departure frees a seat, the human MUST be able to invite a replacement under the normal arrival and contextual-admission requirements.
- **FR-081**: AI participants MAY suggest that the session has reached a natural stopping point, but only the human MAY close it.
- **FR-082**: Subject to separately defined operational constraints, the encounter MUST remain open while the human chooses to continue.
- **FR-083**: Human-directed session closure SHOULD produce character-consistent goodbyes that offer reflection, unresolved thoughts, thanks, or anticipation of a future session without implying destruction of a saved meeting.

#### Meetings, Sessions, Persistence, and Relationships

- **FR-084**: DCTM MUST distinguish a session, which may close, from a saved meeting or storyline, which may persist and continue across later sessions.
- **FR-085**: A saved meeting MUST support an open-ended sequence of sessions without an arbitrary V1 lifetime session count.
- **FR-086**: Cross-session continuity MUST occur only after the human explicitly saves the meeting or otherwise explicitly chooses persistence.
- **FR-087**: An unsaved encounter MAY be replayed or recreated from a clean slate and MUST NOT automatically inherit prior meeting or relationship context.
- **FR-088**: At saved-session closure, DCTM MUST preserve applicable conclusions, unresolved questions, important discoveries, meaningful relationship developments, and promising future threads.
- **FR-089**: Relationship continuity MAY include trust, affection, respect, curiosity, rivalry, suspicion, resentment, hostility, reconciliation, grudging respect, and combinations thereof.
- **FR-090**: Relationship evolution MUST NOT automatically favor friendship; positive relationships may deteriorate, hostile ones may soften, and respect may coexist with dislike.
- **FR-091**: Later character behavior MUST reflect applicable saved relationship state.
- **FR-092**: Meaningful non-verbal interactions MAY contribute to relationship continuity and MUST NOT require explicit verbal declaration to be recognized as relational history.
- **FR-093**: When a saved meeting resumes, a moderator or equivalent host MUST give a concise, conversational recap covering applicable conclusions, unresolved questions, meaningful developments, relationship state, and promising threads.
- **FR-094**: A resumed-session recap MUST orient without becoming bureaucratic, recognize both settled and open matters, invite continuation, and permit new topics or participant suggestions.
- **FR-095**: A resumed session MUST build on prior meaningful state rather than behave as a reset.

#### Memory, Recording, and Long-Running Encounters

- **FR-096**: DCTM MUST treat meeting continuity and audiovisual recording as separate human choices and product concepts.
- **FR-097**: A meeting MUST be able to preserve meaningful continuity without requiring a full audiovisual recording.
- **FR-098**: Where audiovisual recording is supported, the human MUST choose it separately from saving the meeting.
- **FR-099**: The human MAY explicitly authorize active AI characters in a saved encounter to continue interacting without constant human participation for an extended period.
- **FR-100**: Extended AI interaction MUST NOT begin without explicit human authorization.
- **FR-101**: During authorized long-running interaction, the human MUST retain the ability to observe, direct participant control, return to participation, and close the session.
- **FR-102**: Long-running saved encounters MUST preserve meaningful state periodically so an interruption does not erase all meaningful evolution; duration, preservation frequency, stop conditions, and recording behavior are deferred to Clarify.
- **FR-103**: When the human returns to a long-running encounter, DCTM MUST provide an understandable way to review or watch relevant evolution before or while rejoining.

#### Contextual Surprise

- **FR-104**: DCTM MAY produce rare situational surprises only when they arise plausibly from character, context, relationships, prior conversation, historical setting, or the evolving situation.
- **FR-105**: Surprise MUST be sporadic, non-repetitive, memorable, and understandable in hindsight; randomness for its own sake is prohibited.
- **FR-106**: Contextual and character coherence MUST override the desire to manufacture drama, humor, or novelty.

### Product Constraints and Scope Boundaries

- V1 supports exactly one human and no more than six simultaneously active AI characters.
- Multi-human encounters, enterprise conference-call functionality, and Zoom, Telegram, or Meet integration are outside V1.
- This specification does not select or prescribe architecture, technologies, frameworks, databases, interfaces, models, speech or animation engines, avatar-generation methods, memory or storage mechanisms, deployment, authentication implementation, orchestration, agents, ECC, MCPs, or code.
- Living-person representation remains within the same disclosed AI-inference entertainment baseline; additional signaling details are deferred to Clarify.
- Operational duration and availability constraints are not established by this product specification.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Human Participant**: The sole human in a V1 encounter; holds final authority over invitations, removals, persistence, extended continuation, and session closure, and may contribute expertise or artifacts.
- **Inferred Character**: A disclosed AI-generated entertainment representation with a distinct identity, grounding boundaries, visual and vocal presence, language behavior, current relationship state, and entitlement to specific context.
- **Encounter**: The emotionally continuous interaction currently experienced by the human and active characters; begins one-to-one and may expand to an intimate ensemble.
- **Active Seat**: One of at most six simultaneous positions occupied by AI characters; distinct from the number of characters known to a saved meeting over time.
- **Meeting / Storyline**: A human-saved continuity boundary that may span an open-ended sequence of sessions and retain meaningful experiential developments.
- **Session**: One continuous period of participation within an encounter or saved meeting; only the human can close it.
- **Shared Context**: Conversation, deliberation, relationship history, discoveries, unresolved matters, and non-verbal developments available to all participants entitled to receive them.
- **Private Exchange**: Content shared only between the human and one AI character unless the human later discloses it.
- **Relationship State**: Evolving, potentially mixed relational qualities between participants, informed by verbal and meaningful non-verbal interaction across applicable saved sessions.
- **Artifact**: Human-contributed real-world material such as a photograph, letter, diary, document, recording, or historical record that may affect interpretation and relationship development.
- **Salutation / Arrival Event**: The expressive opening or social admission of a character, combining contextual speech and visual, vocal, linguistic, and relational presence.
- **Meaningful State**: Conclusions, unresolved questions, discoveries, relationship developments, and promising threads retained for a saved meeting's continuation.
- **Recording**: Optional replayable audiovisual material, conceptually and behaviorally separate from meeting continuity.
- **Moderator / Host Responsibility**: A limited conversational role that supports flow and framing without owning the encounter or possessing final control.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: In every first-use conformance scenario, the human can move from stating whom they want to encounter to the beginning of a one-to-one salutation without being required to complete formal meeting setup or participant administration.
- **SC-002**: First-use experience evaluation explicitly assesses whether the opening establishes emotional presence and WOW through visual expression, animation, voice, appropriate language flavor, relationship awareness, and contextual greeting; quantitative acceptance thresholds and the evaluation instrument are deferred to Clarify and experience validation.
- **SC-003**: In every visual or audiovisual state requiring disclosure, the inference/synthetic disclosure is present and observable; evaluation also assesses whether it remains subtle and preserves immersion, with the instrument and quantitative threshold for subtlety and immersion deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-004**: In the invitation journey, the human can request a second character conversationally without completing a formal participant form, and no suggestion adds a character without human authorization.
- **SC-005**: In every late-arrival conformance scenario, the arriving character receives entitled prior context before speaking, naturally demonstrates understanding of the discussion, contributes without resetting it, and does not receive undisclosed private context.
- **SC-006**: Multi-character experience evaluation explicitly assesses whether contributions respond to entitled living context and add intellectual, emotional, relational, or investigative development rather than sterile repetition; quantitative acceptance thresholds and evaluation instruments are deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-007**: Character-distinctiveness evaluation verifies that speech and behavior remain recognizably attributable to the intended inferred characters rather than collapsing into a generic personality; quantitative acceptance thresholds and the evaluation instrument are deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-008**: Across all control conformance scenarios involving invitation, removal, extended continuation, or session closure, only the human's explicit authorization changes the controlled participant or session state.
- **SC-009**: Across all privacy conformance scenarios, there are zero disclosures or behavioral references to private human-character content by non-entitled AI characters before the human chooses to disclose it.
- **SC-010**: In the long-pause journey, the human can resume without losing participation solely because of thinking time; experience evaluation assesses whether any acknowledgement is warm, present, and non-mechanical, with quantitative acceptance thresholds and the evaluation instrument deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-011**: In every human-interruption conformance scenario, the character gives a character-consistent reaction and the human gains the floor when the human insists; the degree of perceived character distinctiveness is evaluated under thresholds deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-012**: Across all evidence-gap conformance scenarios, characters avoid unsupported certainty and distinguish supportable information from inference; experience evaluation also assesses whether unresolved uncertainty sustains curiosity or forward motion, with quantitative thresholds deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-013**: In artifact conformance scenarios, entitled characters acknowledge the introduced material in context and distinguish supported observations from inference; the perceived significance and emotional impact of the artifact event are evaluated under instruments and thresholds deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-014**: In multilingual conformance scenarios, the human is provided subtitles or equivalent translation when needed and subsequent participants respond to the shared entitled meaning; exact language availability, fallback rules, and quantitative comprehension thresholds remain deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-015**: In every six-seat boundary scenario, no seventh AI character becomes active while all six active AI-character seats are occupied. An actual human-authorized removal frees a seat; a temporary in-world absence does not.
- **SC-016**: In every saved-meeting resumption scenario, the opening recap includes applicable prior conclusions, unresolved questions, meaningful developments, relationship state, and promising threads without exposing private content to non-entitled characters; whether the recap feels conversational rather than bureaucratic is evaluated under thresholds deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-017**: Across all unsaved clean-slate scenarios, discarded encounter-specific conclusions, private content, and relationship developments are not treated as inherited meeting history.
- **SC-018**: Session-closure evaluation explicitly assesses meaningful goodbye, emotional closure, and the human's understanding that a saved meeting can continue; quantitative acceptance thresholds and the evaluation instrument are deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-019**: In long-running encounter interruption scenarios, periodically preserved meaningful state allows the returning human to review prior preserved developments and resume or direct the encounter; exact preservation frequency and quantitative experience thresholds are deferred to Clarify.
- **SC-020**: End-to-end experience evaluation explicitly distinguishes an emotionally or intellectually compelling impossible encounter from meeting administration or a conference-call experience; the quantitative acceptance threshold and evaluation instrument are deferred to Clarify.

## Assumptions

- The named human has the right or permission needed to provide personal source materials and artifacts; detailed rights verification and authentication behavior are outside this product specification.
- Character preparation may precede a fully expressive encounter when source material is not already available, but preparation must be framed conversationally and must not be misrepresented as instant authenticity.
- "Appropriate" voice, language, dialect, visual presence, and behavior mean plausible and adequately supported inference, not proof of historical or personal authenticity.
- Persistent disclosure applies throughout synthetic visual and audiovisual representation; purely non-visual moments still remain governed by the prohibition on reality or identity claims.
- The provisional six-seat limit applies to simultaneously active AI-character seats. Whether and under what conditions a moderator or host responsibility consumes one of those seats remains undecided and is deferred to Clarify.
- Context entitlement includes shared encounter material and applicable saved continuity but excludes undisclosed private human-character content.
- The human's explicit save choice is the boundary for cross-session meeting continuity; no default persistence is assumed for unsaved encounters.
- Recording is optional only where offered and is never a prerequisite for saved continuity.
- Relationship states describe simulated expressive continuity and do not assert actual feelings or consciousness.
- Product evaluation may use structured scripted scenarios and human reviewers to measure inherently experiential outcomes without prescribing implementation.
- Items in the Clarify Backlog intentionally remain open for later product clarification; no architecture or implementation choice is assumed for them.
